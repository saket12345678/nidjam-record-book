# NIDJAM Record Book

Searchable record of AFI's National Inter District Junior Athletics Meet (NIDJAM), all 19 editions, 2003–2024.

**Live page:** https://saket12345678.github.io/nidjam-record-book/

- Finals (all placings) for the 15 editions with published results, plus the full 2024 heats and semi-finals
- Meet records set at the 2nd (2004), 3rd (2005), 8th (2010) and 13th (2015) editions, recovered from record lines in later AFI results files
- 2,863 major athletes (top-8 finalists and meet-record setters), champions by edition, meet records, state medal table

Source: official AFI results files on indianathletics.in; each row on the page links to its file.
